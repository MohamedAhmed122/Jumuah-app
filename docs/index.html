Islamo Centras — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026 · Last updated 31 August 2026

This policy explains how the Islamo Centras mobile app collects, uses and protects information. It applies to the app only — the website islamocentras.lt has its own separate privacy policy.

Data controller: VšĮ Islamo Centras, a non-profit public institution (viešoji įstaiga) registered in the Republic of Lithuania, registration No. 306229208, registered address Rugių g. 1A, Vilnius, Lithuania.
Contact: info@islamocentras.lt

1. Scope of this policy

This policy governs the Islamo Centras mobile application only (Android and iOS, package/bundle identifier com.islamo.centras). It does not cover the islamocentras.lt website, including its shop and "Support Us" donation pages, which are governed by the website's own privacy policy at islamocentras.lt/en/privacy-policy, or any third-party service you open from the app, such as an external maps application for directions.

2. No account, no payments

The app does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or phone number in order to use it. The app has no purchases, subscriptions, advertising, or payment functionality of any kind, and it does not process card details, bank information or donations. If you wish to donate or make a purchase, this can only be done through the islamocentras.lt website, under its own privacy policy and payment provider's terms — never inside the app.

3. Information we collect

Location (optional)

If you grant location permission, your coordinates are used only on your device to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction and distances to mosques and Halal places. Location access is optional — if declined, the app uses Vilnius as a default. Your coordinates are processed on-device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

App/device identifier

The app generates a random identifier stored on your device. It is sent to our backend only when needed to register for notifications or record event participation, and it is not linked to your name or any other directly identifying information.

Push notification data

If you enable notifications, an Expo push token, your selected language and your selected mosque identifier(s) are sent to our backend so we can deliver Adhan reminders and community notifications.

Mosque selection

The mosque(s) you choose are stored on your device and, if you enable notifications or announcements, are sent to our backend to filter content to your chosen mosque(s).

Event participation

If you join or leave a community event, your app/device identifier, language and mosque identifier are sent to our backend to record attendance and manage capacity.

Data kept only on your device

Your prayer history, missed-prayer (Qada) counters, quiz scores, app preferences and cached community content are stored locally using on-device storage. This data is never uploaded to our servers and is not visible to us.

4. What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Payment, banking or donation information
  • An advertising identifier, and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Photos, camera, microphone, contacts, or biometric data
  • Your personal prayer-tracking history (this stays on your device)

5. How we use this information

  • Calculate prayer times, Qibla direction and nearby-place distances
  • Deliver the prayer, Adhan and community notifications you request
  • Show announcements and events for the mosque(s) you follow
  • Record your event RSVP and manage attendee capacity
  • Keep the app usable offline through local caching

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Legal basis for processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, we process the data above on the following bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — for location access and notification permissions, which you grant through your device's permission prompts and can withdraw at any time in system settings.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — in operating the features you actively choose to use, such as syncing your selected mosque, delivering notifications you requested, and processing event RSVPs you initiate.

7. Sharing and third-party processors

We do not sell your information and do not use it for third-party advertising. We share limited information only with processors who handle it on our behalf, under our instructions, to operate the features above:

  • Expo Application Services (650 Industries, Inc.) — delivers push notifications using the push token described in Section 3.
  • Our backend hosting infrastructure — stores the app/device identifier, language, mosque selection and event participation needed to deliver notifications and event features.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of Islamo Centras, our users, or the public.

8. International data transfers

Some of our service providers, including Expo Application Services, may process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where this occurs, we rely on the safeguards required under GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your information.

9. Data retention

Data stored only on your device (prayer history, Qada counters, quiz scores, preferences, cached content) remains there until you delete the app or use "Reset application data" in Settings, which permanently removes it from your device. Server-side records (app/device identifier, push token, mosque selection, event participation) are retained only for as long as needed to provide notifications and event features, and are deleted or anonymized once no longer needed or upon a deletion request.

10. Data security

Communication between the app and our backend uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Sensitive settings on your device are stored using secure, encrypted device storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

11. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of the Republic of Lithuania (ada.lt), or your local supervisory authority

Because the app does not collect your name or email, most of these rights can be exercised directly by deleting the app or using "Reset application data" in Settings. To request deletion of server-side records tied to your app/device identifier, contact us at info@islamocentras.lt.

12. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general community use and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
